# Linger 0.5.5: phone notifications, and a clearer voice on the desktop

**Phones can notify you now**: mentions, DMs, knocks, people you follow and
rooms you pick, each switched on by you, server by server, and never as a
number on the icon. **Phones stay in voice when locked** or with another app
in front. And **the desktop app sounds better in a call**: each microphone is
brought to a normal speaking level and cleaned of background noise, somebody
on speakers no longer sends your voice back to you, and Settings can play
your microphone back the way the others hear it. Around those: a list at rest
no longer works the computer hard, scrolling up a long conversation on Linux
no longer goes blank, and quitting shows you offline at once.

**Everyone: update the app.** With the setup.exe or the AppImage, Linger
installs it itself a day after it's published, or now with **Restart now** at
the foot of the list or in Settings → Account & App. The `.deb`, `.rpm` and
`.msi` download it and ask, with **Update** at the foot of the list. On
Android, update from Google Play or install the new `.apk`; on an iPhone,
TestFlight offers it. See [Install or update](#install-or-update).

**Hosts: update the server** with `./update.sh`, as usual. Phone
notifications need it: an app on an older server says the server needs an
update. There's one small database change, which `update.sh` backs up first.
Nothing in `compose.yaml` changes. See
[For hosts](#for-hosts-updating-the-server).

## The highlights

- **Phone notifications** on Android and the iPhone (#830), encrypted so only
  your phone can read them.
- **Phones stay in voice when locked** or in the background (#851).
- **A clearer voice from the desktop app**: levelled, cleaned, and no echo
  from speakers (#904, #906), with a **microphone test** in Settings (#907).
- **On Linux, the desktop's own microphones and speakers** in Settings
  (#905).
- **A list at rest no longer works the computer hard** (#950).
- **Linger is for people 13 and older**, and the phone app checks the store's
  age where a law asks for it (#953).

## Phones

- **Notifications** (#830) are off until you turn them on, server by server:
  **Settings → Notifications**, or hold a room's name in the list and tap
  **Turn on notifications**. Choose mentions, DMs, knocks, people you follow,
  and rooms you pick; "only mentions" lets only mentions through, and the
  phone's Do Not Disturb holds them all. Tapping one opens that conversation.
  Your server encrypts each notification so only your phone can open it, and
  sends it through Linger's relay (`push.linger.im`) to Apple or Google,
  which carry it without being able to read it. Your next knock replaces
  your last on every phone, rather than piling up (#898). On Android,
  notifications need Google Play services.
- **Voice keeps going with the phone locked** or another app in front, on
  the iPhone and Android (#851). A phone call takes priority. If voice ends
  while Linger is in the background, the list and the voice strip say so,
  with one tap to join again; it never rejoins by itself.
- **No bar over the iPhone's keyboard** (#881). The message box sits right on
  the keyboard, so Send is never half hidden. A finger dragging anywhere, or
  a tap anywhere but a field or a button, puts the keyboard away.
- **Linger is for people 13 and older** (#953). Some US states (Texas so far)
  make the app stores share the age they hold with apps. Where the store says
  so, the phone app asks it, and somebody it says is under 13 sees one screen
  saying Linger is for people 13 and older, and is signed out. Everywhere
  else nothing changes and nobody is asked anything. The age stays on the
  phone: it's never sent anywhere or kept.
- **Android 17** (#842): the app targets it. A server at a home address asks
  once for **Nearby devices**, which Android now requires to reach your own
  network. Joining voice on a phone that hadn't allowed the microphone no
  longer closes the app.
- **The call bar shows on Android again** (#834). In 0.5.4's release build
  joining voice never showed it, and voice might not keep going with the
  phone locked, because the build's shrinking removed the code Linger calls.
  A check now reads every release build for it.
- **The iPhone app no longer keeps the phone busy** while it's open, which
  warmed it and used battery in TestFlight builds since early November
  (#901). Testers only: nobody else had those builds.
- **Under the hood**: Tauri 2.12, the iPhone app on UIKit's scene life cycle,
  and built with Xcode 27.1 and the iOS 27 SDK, which Apple requires from
  April 2027 (#836, #837, #838, #839). Nothing is meant to look different.

## Voice on the desktop

- **Your microphone is levelled and cleaned** before it's sent (#904), as the
  phone app's already was, so people on the desktop app are no longer too
  quiet. Everybody you talk with needs 0.5.5 for you to hear the difference
  in them.
- **No echo from speakers** (#906): somebody on speakers no longer sends your
  voice back to you. Each has a switch in Settings → Sound & Voice.
- **A microphone test** (#907) in Settings → Sound & Voice: talk for a few
  seconds and hear it back the way the others hear you.
- **On Linux, Settings lists the microphones and speakers** your desktop's
  sound settings do, by the same names (#905). If you'd picked one before,
  pick it again; until then Linger uses the system default.
- **A voice message called off as it starts no longer keeps recording**
  (#913, #920). Closing the window, pressing Escape or Discard, or opening
  another conversation while the microphone opens now calls it off, on a
  computer and on a phone.

## Conversations and the list

- **A list at rest no longer works the computer hard** (#950). A Shimmer
  name's light used to cross it forever, which on Linux kept about half a
  CPU core and the graphics card busy. The light now crosses three times when
  that person arrives and once under the mouse, then rests.
- **Scrolling up a long conversation on Linux no longer goes blank** (#944).
  A rare one-frame jump is left.
- **Back to the newest shows whenever you've scrolled well up** (#945), more
  than three screens above the newest message, and pressing it leaves the
  cursor in the message box.
- **Quitting Linger shows you offline at once** (#942), rather than 5 seconds
  later, and in a call your seat goes at once rather than up to two minutes
  later. Except on Windows when the updater restarts Linger.
- **An installed copy passes over a dev build's sign-in** to `localhost`
  (#902).

## Known issues

- **Phone notifications are new.** Tell us if one doesn't arrive, arrives
  twice, or opens the wrong place.
- **The iPhone app is still in TestFlight.** It goes to the App Store once
  the testers have tried this release.
- **On Linux, scrolling up can still jump by a frame**, about once in a long
  scroll (#944).
- **A computer with NVIDIA's legacy driver can't watch a screen** (#729).

## Older apps and servers

Nothing stops working with an older app or server (PROTOCOL §9).

- **A 0.5.5 app on a 0.5.4 server** works as before, with every fix in the
  app. Notifications say that server needs an update.
- **A 0.5.4 app on a 0.5.5 server** works as before, without notifications.
- The oldest app a server works with is still 0.4.1.

## For hosts: updating the server

Server image: `ghcr.io/itsmattguenther/linger:0.5.5` (x86-64 only), also
tagged `0.5` and `latest`.

### What changes for you

- **Phone notifications** (#830, #886). Somebody whose phone asks your server
  for notifications gets them through Linger's relay, `push.linger.im`. Your
  server encrypts each one so the relay, Apple and Google can't read it, and
  it never learns where a phone is. No port, no account, no setting: nothing
  is sent until a phone asks. To turn it off for your whole server, put
  `LINGER_PUSH=off` in `.env` and run `docker compose up -d`. The host guide's
  [Phone notifications](../host-guide.md#phone-notifications) has the rest.
- **One small database change** (migration 0027): a table of the phones that
  asked for notifications, one row per sign-in. Signing out, a new password
  or removing somebody deletes their row. `update.sh` saves the database into
  `backups/` first.
- **No new setting you need**, and nothing in `compose.yaml` changes.

### The steps

On the server, in your `linger` folder:

```bash
cd ~/linger
./update.sh
```

(Use `sudo ./update.sh` if you type `sudo` before `docker` commands.) It
downloads 0.5.5 while the server keeps running, stops it for a few seconds to
save the database into `backups/`, then starts everything again and prints the
version before and after. If 0.5.5 doesn't start, it prints the commands that
put 0.5.4 back.

Check it worked, with your own address in place of `linger.example.com`:

```bash
curl -f https://linger.example.com/api/v1/health
```

It prints `{"ok":true,"version":"0.5.5","oldest_client":"0.4.1"}`.

**Going back to 0.5.4** means restoring the copy `update.sh` saved into
`backups/` just before 0.5.5 started, and anything posted since is lost. The
host guide's [Restoring](../host-guide.md#restoring) says how.

## Install or update

Linger installs this itself, a day after it's published: with the setup.exe
or the AppImage at the next start, or after half an hour with nobody using it,
never while you're in voice. The `.deb`, `.rpm` and `.msi` download it and
ask, with **Update** at the foot of the list. **Restart now** takes it at
once. On Omarchy or Arch, Linger updates with your system (Update in the
Omarchy menu, or `sudo pacman -Syu`). Windows installers are updater-signed,
not Authenticode-signed; SmartScreen may still warn about an unfamiliar app.

On Android, update from Google Play if you're testing there, or download
`Linger_0.5.5_android-arm64.apk` from this release on the phone and install
it over the old one. You stay signed in. On an iPhone, TestFlight offers 0.5.5
once it's through Apple's processing.
