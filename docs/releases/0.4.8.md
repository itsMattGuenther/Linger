# Linger 0.4.8: the phone app

Linger is on Android now: download it from this release, sign in, and your
rooms, DMs and voice messages are in your pocket. On the computer, wireless
headsets that Linger couldn't hear now work, someone talking in a room's voice
shows in that room, the host can take somebody out of voice when they walk
away with their microphone on, and the host can name co-hosts for when they're
away.

**Hosts: update the server.** Report and block, co-hosts, and taking somebody
out of voice need it. **This one changes the database**, so going back to 0.4.7
means restoring the backup `update.sh` makes. [For hosts](#for-hosts-updating-the-server)
has the steps.

**Everyone: update the app.** The headset fix and everything else on the
computer is in the app.

## The highlights

- **The phone app, on Android** (#253). Download
  `Linger_0.4.8_android-arm64.apk` from this release on your phone and open it.
  [The phone app](#the-phone-app) says what it does and doesn't do.
- **Wireless headsets work in voice** (#398). A SteelSeries Arctis Nova 5
  joined voice but Linger never heard it, even after 0.4.7. Its wireless
  receiver reports a tiny glitch every few moments, and Linger took each one
  as "the microphone died" and started it over, so it never heard a word.
  Glitches are now let go, as Discord does.
- **Being in a room's voice is being in that room** (#420). Somebody talking in
  #general with a game in front of them used to drop out of #general and show
  as idle. Now their dot stays on the room and People says "in #general".
- **The host can take somebody out of voice** (#423). For somebody who walked
  away with their headset on and unmuted.
- **Co-hosts** (#424). The host can make somebody a co-host, who can do what
  the host does in the app while the host is away.
- **Report and block** (T-1605), on the computer and the phone.

## The phone app

**Android first.** iPhone comes later, once the Android app has done well.

- **Installing.** On your phone, open this release page, download the file
  ending in `android-arm64.apk`, and open it. Android asks to allow installs
  from your browser the first time: tap **Settings**, turn on **Allow from this
  source**, and go back. That's Android's rule for any app that isn't from the
  Play Store. It needs Android 7 or newer on a 64-bit phone, which is nearly
  every phone from the last eight years.
- **What it does:** rooms, DMs, your status, Media, Search, sending pictures
  from your phone, and voice messages. One screen at a time, with ← Back, the
  way phone chat apps work.
- **What it doesn't do:** voice rooms, and notifications. Nothing wakes it and
  it shows nothing on your lock screen; when you open it, it catches up.
- **Its sounds follow your phone.** Chimes play while the app is open, a short
  buzz on vibrate, and nothing on silent.
- **Updating.** It doesn't update itself yet. For a new version, download the
  new `.apk` from its release page and install it over the old one. You stay
  signed in.
- **Your phone's backups leave Linger out**, so what it keeps never reaches
  Google's cloud.

## Voice

- **Wireless headsets** (#398). See the highlights. This also steadies other
  wireless headsets: before, every glitch made Linger restart the microphone,
  which you'd hear as a short dropout.
- **When the microphone you picked won't open** (#398). If you picked a
  microphone in Settings → Sound & Voice and it won't open (another program
  holding on to it, say), Linger still uses your computer's default so you can
  talk, and now the voice bar says so, with your computer's reason. It used to
  switch without a word.
- **Taking somebody out of voice** (#423), for the host. Click their name in
  the voice bar, or open their card and its **···**, and choose **Take out of
  voice**. They leave the call as if they'd pressed Leave, their app says they
  were taken out of voice, and they can join again whenever they're back. It isn't a
  mute: nobody can mute anybody else or turn anybody's microphone on.
- **Voice messages from the phone** (#401). Recorded on a phone, they play on
  the computer, and the other way round. Android asks for the microphone the
  first time you press Record.

## Rooms and people

- **Co-hosts** (#424). Open somebody's card, its **···**, and choose **Make
  Jules a co-host**. A co-host can do what the host does in the app: rooms,
  the server's name and color, invites, reports, removing and restoring
  members, deleting messages, and taking somebody out of voice. Two things
  stay the host's: making or removing co-hosts, and anything done to the host
  themselves. Their card says "co-host". Undo it from the same menu. A
  co-host never sees a report about themselves.

- **In a room's voice is in the room** (#420). Their dot shows on the room for
  as long as they're in its voice, and People says "in #general", not "idle".
  Reading another room while they talk, they show in both. An away they set
  themselves still shows.
- **Report and block** (T-1605).
  - **Report** a message (last in its actions) or a person (their card's
    **···**) to the host, with a note if you like. It goes to the host and
    nobody else, and the person isn't told.
  - **Block** somebody from their card. Their messages fold into a grey line
    you can open, their DMs never light up or chime, their knocks stop
    reaching you, and nothing of theirs shows in Media or Search. They aren't
    told. Unblock any time, there or in Settings → Account.
  - **The host** sees one quiet row at the top of the list when there's a
    report to look at, never a number, and handles it in Settings → People.
- **Pins on pictures and links** (T-908). A pinned picture, file, voice
  message or link now shows its pin beside it. A link's pin didn't show at
  all, and a picture's sat above it.

## Under the hood

- Each release now carries a signed Android app, checked against the release

- The Windows installer check gives up on a stuck installer after five minutes

## Known issues

- **The phone app doesn't update itself.** Install each new `.apk` over the
  old one.
- **On Hyprland (Omarchy included), the mouse pointer jumps into a
  conversation that opens in a window of its own.** The
  [user guide](../user-guide.md#worth-knowing) has the two lines that turn it
  off.
- **The AppImage doesn't play AV1 videos** (#358). Windows and the Arch,
  `.deb` and `.rpm` packages do.

## For hosts: updating the server

Server image: `ghcr.io/itsmattguenther/linger:0.4.8` (x86-64 only), also
tagged `latest`.

**What changes for you:**

- **A database change** (report and block, and co-hosts). `update.sh` saves
  the database into `backups/` before it starts 0.4.8, and that backup is how
  you'd go back.
- **Reports come to you.** One quiet row at the top of your list says there's
  one to look at; Settings → People shows who sent it and about what, and
  **Let it go** or **Delete message** closes it.
- **You can take somebody out of voice** (#423).
- **You can name co-hosts** (#424) from a person's card, for when you're away.
  Updating the server stays yours: the app has nothing to do with that.
- **No new setting.** ARM servers stay on 0.4.4.

Run these **on the server**, one block at a time.

**1. Go to the folder with your `compose.yaml`** (the `linger` folder from
setup):

```bash
cd ~/linger
```

**2. Update:**

```bash
./update.sh
```

(Use `sudo ./update.sh` if you type `sudo` before `docker` commands. No
`update.sh` in the folder? The [0.4.5 notes](0.4.5.md#for-hosts-updating-the-server)
show how to get it.) It downloads 0.4.8 while the server keeps running, stops
it for a few seconds to save the database into `backups/`, starts everything
again, and prints the version before and after.

**3. Check it worked:**

```bash
curl -f https://linger.example.com/api/v1/health
```

With your own address in place of `linger.example.com`, it should print
`{"ok":true,"version":"0.4.8"}`.

**If something goes wrong**, `update.sh` prints the commands that put 0.4.7
back **with the backup it just made**. Going back needs that backup: 0.4.7
won't start on a database 0.4.8 has changed. Anything sent after the update
is lost when you go back, so decide soon if you're going to.

## Install or update

Use the in-app update: **Update…** at the foot of the list, or Settings →
Account & App → Updates. Windows users should keep the same installer format
(`.exe` or `.msi`) when upgrading. Linux packages are `.AppImage`, `.deb` and
`.rpm`; on Omarchy or Arch, Linger updates with your system. Windows
installers are updater-signed, not Authenticode-signed; SmartScreen may still
warn about an unfamiliar app. The Android app installs from this release page
(see [The phone app](#the-phone-app)).

## Checks on installed clients

Record OS, package type, previous and new version, and headset or microphone
with any failure. Use made-up messages, and keep private conversations out of
screenshots.

1. **Windows, a SteelSeries Arctis Nova 5** (or another wireless headset):
   join voice. Your name lights when you talk and others hear you (#398).
2. **Android:** install the `.apk` on a phone that never had Linger, sign in
   with an invite, send a message and a picture, record a voice message and
   play it on a computer.
3. **Talking in a room's voice with Linger in the background** for a few
   minutes: your dot stays on the room for others, and People says
   "in #room", not idle (#420).
4. **As the host:** take somebody out of voice from the voice bar. They leave,
   their room's voice line says they were taken out, and they can join again
   (#423).
5. **Report** a message to the host and **block** somebody, on the computer
   and on the phone. The host sees the report row and closes it.
6. **Pin** a picture and a link: the pin shows beside each.
7. **Pick a microphone in Settings that another program is holding**, then
   join: the voice bar says it wouldn't open, and why.
8. **As the host:** make somebody a co-host from their card. They see the
   report row and can take somebody out of voice; they can't make co-hosts.
   Undo it (#424).
