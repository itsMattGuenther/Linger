# Linger 0.4.4: a big one

A day of real use turned into the biggest release since the Buddy list
arrived. You can now @ people by the name you know them by and paste
screenshots straight in. DMs you haven't read are hard to miss. Your status
has fields you label yourself. The list shows at a glance who's actually
here, and voice now always goes through the server.

**Hosts: update the server, and make sure voice is set up first.** Voice now
needs `LINGER_VOICE_ADDRESS`. A server without it carries no voice at all
after this update. The database also changes, so take a backup first.
[For hosts](#for-hosts-updating-the-server) has the exact commands.

**Everyone: update the app.** Apps older than 0.4.1 can no longer join voice.

## The highlights

- **@ opens a list of people**, found by the name you know them by (#267).
- **Paste a picture** into the message box with Ctrl+V (#276).
- **A DM you haven't read is lit**, pops a banner and flashes the taskbar (#291).
- **Status fields with your own labels**, and links in them that open (#270).
- **Lights off for idle, away and offline**: only people who are here are lit (#301).
- **A sender's name gets its own line**, so every message starts on one edge (#295).
- **Voice always goes through the server**, and sound settings apply mid-call
  (#306, #249).

## Chat

- **Mention people by the name you know** (#267). Type `@` at the start of a
  word and a list of people opens by the message box. Search by display name
  or username. Up and Down move through it, Enter, Tab or a click picks, and
  Escape closes it. In a room it offers everyone on the server, with the
  people in the room first; in a DM, only the people in it. When you read a
  mention, it shows the person's display name, with their @username on hover.
- **Paste a picture from the clipboard** (#276). A screenshot, or Copy Image
  from a browser or image viewer, goes onto your message as a file, the same
  as picking it with +. Plain text pastes as before. This never worked on
  Linux until now.
- **A sender's name on its own line** (#295). Messages used to start right
  after "Name:", so a longer name pushed that person's words further in. Now
  the name sits on its own line, and everybody's words start on one edge
  just below it. A new speaker is easy to spot.
- **Line breaks and indentation are kept** (#289). Lines typed with
  Shift+Enter used to run together into one.
- **A very long message folds after twenty lines** (#304), fading out, with
  **Show all** under it and **Show less** once it's open. Only the words fold,
  never a picture. Copying and screen readers still get all of it.
- **Back to the newest** no longer loads the same page twice (#266).

## DMs and notifications

- **A DM you haven't read is lit** (#291), not just bold. Its row gets a soft
  fill and edge, and so do the DMs heading and the server's header while
  they're folded, and its tab when another tab is showing. Reading it puts
  the light out. Rooms stay bold only.
- **Every DM gets a desktop banner** saying who it's from, and Linger's
  taskbar button flashes until you look (Windows flashes the button, Linux
  marks the window as wanting attention). Nothing happens for a DM you're
  already reading. Turn both off in **Settings → Notifications → Banners for
  DMs**.
- **DMs stay in order of the latest message** while Linger runs (#248). They
  used to go back to the order they had when Linger started, once read.

## People and status

- **The lights are off for idle, away and offline** (#301, #274). Somebody
  who's here has their name in their own style and a solid dot. Everybody
  else's name is a dim grey, and their mark says which: a half-strength dot
  for idle, a half-strength moon for away, a hollow dot for offline. Away
  messages keep their warm color. Cards, conversations and your own name are
  unchanged.
- **Status fields with your own labels** (#270). A status had three fixed
  fields: Listening to, Reading and Working on. Now you get up to three, and
  you pick each label: Listening to, Reading, Working on, Playing, Watching,
  or your own (24 characters). A web address in a field opens in your
  browser. Your existing status carries over as it was.
- **A status is words now; status pictures are gone** (#269). Saving one
  often failed, and the Buddy list never showed them. Share a photo in a room
  instead, where people see it and Media keeps it. Existing status pictures
  are cleared when the server updates.
- **See your own card the way friends see it** (#271). Click your own name,
  at the top of the list or on one of your messages. **Edit profile** takes
  the place of Message and Knock.
- **Display names refuse invisible and control characters** (#296): line
  breaks, tabs, direction controls, invisible characters outside emoji, piles
  of accent marks, or nothing visible at all. Emoji, accents and every
  language still work. Names saved before this keep working.
- **Names paint the same everywhere** (#272, #287). A gradient name on a
  person's card showed only its first color, and a glowing name's light
  stopped at a hard edge in the list and in voice. Both are fixed.

## Voice and sound

- **Voice always goes through the server** (#306). It has carried every call
  since 0.4.1, with the older direct way kept as a fallback. That fallback is
  gone, and so is the **Voice through the server** switch in Settings. A
  room holds up to 25 people. A server that isn't set up for voice now says
  **"Voice isn't set up on this server"** instead of offering a call nobody
  could hear.
- **Changing your microphone or speakers applies to the call you're in**
  (#249). You no longer have to leave and rejoin.
- **When voice won't start, it says why** (#261, #273), on the room's voice
  line, and **Start talking** stays there for another go. If your default
  microphone or speakers won't open, it says so, with a **Pick yours in
  Settings** button. More microphones and headsets open than before.
- **Linger's own sounds play on the Speakers you picked** (#250). Chimes,
  knocks and the voice sounds used to go to the system default, even with
  voice on your headset.
- **The knock is twice as loud**, and mute and unmute 1.6 times (#252). They
  were the hardest sounds to hear on laptop speakers.

## Knocks

- **Knock says why it can't** (#288). While somebody is offline, Knock stays
  greyed out and says "Can't knock while Jen is offline." It comes back the
  moment they're online. A refused knock from a DM says why.
- **The knock limit says when you can knock again** (#268): "Three knocks
  this hour. You can knock again in 20 minutes."
- **Windows: a knock no longer flashes scroll bars** on the list (#251).

## Media, links and invites

- **Shared audio gets Linger's own player** (#247), with a volume slider, the
  same on Linux and Windows. Linux had no way to turn a shared audio file
  down. The last volume you set is kept for the next file.
- **A YouTube link's card shows the video's title** (#300), not just
  "youtube.com". Needs the updated server.
- **An invite for Anyone has no limit** (#246). It used to run out after the
  first person joined. Needs the updated server. Invites made as Anyone
  before the update stay single-use, so make a new one.

## Gone in this release

- **The previous look** (`LINGER_CLASSIC=1`) is deleted (#306). The Buddy list
  has been the app since 0.4.0.
- **Voice straight between people's computers** (#306). Voice goes through the
  server only.
- **Status pictures** (#269).

## Known issues

- **On Hyprland (Omarchy included), opening a conversation moves the mouse
  pointer into the chat window.** That's Hyprland's own behaviour when an app
  brings a window forward, and Linger leaves it to your desktop. The
  [user guide](../user-guide.md#worth-knowing) has the two lines that turn it
  off, if you'd rather it didn't.

## For hosts: updating the server

Server image: `ghcr.io/itsmattguenther/linger:0.4.4` (amd64 and arm64), also
tagged `latest`.

**What changes for you:**

- **Voice needs `LINGER_VOICE_ADDRESS`.** Before, a server without it fell back
  to voice straight between people's computers. Now it carries no voice at
  all. If you already run voice through the server (you set this up for
  0.4.1), nothing changes.
- **The database changes** (two migrations, run by themselves at startup).
  Status pictures are cleared, and statuses move to the new fields. An older
  server won't start on the updated database, so **the backup is your only
  way back** to 0.4.3.
- **No new settings.**

Run these **on the server**, one block at a time.

**1. Go to the folder with your `compose.yaml`** (the `linger` folder from
setup):

```bash
cd ~/linger
```

**2. Check voice is set up:**

```bash
grep -nE '^\s*#?\s*LINGER_VOICE_ADDRESS:|3479:3479' compose.yaml
```

You want two lines back: `LINGER_VOICE_ADDRESS:` with an IP address and
**no `#` in front of it**, and `- "3479:3479/udp"`. If either is missing or
starts with `#`, set up voice now, before updating, or voice stops working
after the update:

```bash
curl -4 https://api.ipify.org
nano compose.yaml
```

The first command prints your server's public IP address. In `compose.yaml`,
remove the `#` before `LINGER_VOICE_ADDRESS` and put that address after it. A
`compose.yaml` from before 0.4.1 has neither line, so add both to the `linger`
service:

```yaml
    environment:
      # ...what's there already...
      LINGER_VOICE_ADDRESS: 203.0.113.7
    ports:
      - "3479:3479/udp"
```

Then open UDP port 3479, in your cloud provider's firewall and on the machine:

```bash
sudo ufw allow 3479/udp
```

**3. Check the image name.** `grep image: compose.yaml` should show
`ghcr.io/itsmattguenther/linger:latest`. If it says `matthewguenther`, change
that line to `ghcr.io/itsmattguenther/linger:latest` with `nano compose.yaml`.

**4. Back up** (a few seconds of downtime):

```bash
docker compose stop linger
tar czf linger-backup-$(date +%F).tar.gz data
```

**5. Update:**

```bash
docker compose pull
docker compose up -d
```

If you run the voice relay (coturn), use these instead:

```bash
docker compose --profile voice pull
docker compose --profile voice up -d
```

**6. Check it worked:**

```bash
docker compose logs linger | grep -i "voice forwarding is on"
curl -f https://linger.example.com/health
```

The first should print a line. The second, with your own address in place of
`linger.example.com`, should print `{"ok":true,"version":"0.4.4"}`.

**If something goes wrong**, go back to 0.4.3 by putting the backup back.
`ls linger-backup-*` shows the backup's name, with the date you made it:

```bash
docker compose down
mv data data-0.4.4-broken
tar xzf linger-backup-2026-09-28.tar.gz
```

(Use your backup's date in the last line.) Then change the image line in
`compose.yaml` to
`ghcr.io/itsmattguenther/linger:0.4.3` and run `docker compose up -d` (with
`--profile voice` if you run the relay).

## Install or update

Use the in-app update: **Update…** at the foot of the list, or Settings →
Account & App → Updates. Windows users should keep the same installer format
(`.exe` or `.msi`) when upgrading. Linux packages are `.AppImage`, `.deb` and
`.rpm`; on Omarchy or Arch, Linger updates with your system. Windows
installers are updater-signed, not Authenticode-signed; SmartScreen may still
warn about an unfamiliar app.

## Checks on installed clients

Record OS, package type, previous and new version, and interface size with
any failure. Use made-up messages, and keep private conversations out of
screenshots.

1. With the server on 0.4.4, join voice in a room from Linux and from
   Windows: you hear each other. Switch microphone or speakers in Settings
   mid-call: the call carries on, on the new device.
2. Type `@` and part of a friend's display name: they're in the list. Enter
   puts in their @username; the sent message shows their display name.
3. Copy a screenshot and press Ctrl+V in the message box, on Linux and on
   Windows: it's added as a file and sends.
4. Get a DM while Linger isn't focused: its row is lit, a banner names who
   sent it, and the taskbar button flashes. Read it: the light goes out. Turn
   off Settings → Notifications → Banners for DMs: the next DM brings neither.
5. In Settings → Profile, give your status a field with your own label and a
   web address as its value. Save, then open your own card: it's there and
   the link opens in your browser.
6. Somebody going idle, away and offline: their name greys, with a
   half-strength dot, a half-strength moon, then a hollow dot. Back here, it's
   lit again.
7. Send a message with three lines typed with Shift+Enter, and one over
   twenty lines: the first keeps its lines, the second folds with Show all.
8. Try to knock on somebody offline: Knock says why. Knock on somebody four
   times in an hour: it says when you can knock again.
9. Make an invite for Anyone and have two people join with it.
10. Share an audio file: its player has a volume slider, on Linux too.
11. Post a YouTube link: its card shows the video's title.
