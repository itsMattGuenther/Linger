# Linger 0.5.4: Linger updates itself, and phones share straight to it

From this release on, **Linger keeps itself up to date**: a day after a
release comes out, the app downloads it and installs it when it won't get in
anybody's way. And **phones can share pictures and files straight to
Linger**: pick Linger in the phone's share sheet. On an iPhone, Linger's own
window rises inside the sheet, and Send posts to the room or person you pick
without leaving the app you shared from. Around those, a busy week of fixes:
everybody in voice knows when a screen share starts, the voice strip fits
everybody it can, a long message you send stays on screen whole, and a short
drop in a busy call no longer leaves you out of voice.

**Everyone: update the app.** **This update is installed the old way**, since
the app that installs updates by itself is this one: in 0.5.3, **Update…** at
the foot of the list, or Settings → Account & App → **Install and restart**.
From 0.5.4 on, Linger does it for you.

**Hosts: update the server** with `./update.sh`, as usual. This one has a
small database change, which `update.sh` backs up first. The server writes
the line saying somebody shared their screen, gives the iPhone's share window
its sign-in, keeps the last two minutes for somebody whose connection dropped,
and tells apps the oldest version it works with. Nothing in `compose.yaml`
changes. See [For hosts](#for-hosts-updating-the-server).

## The highlights

- **Linger updates itself** (#772), and an app too old for a server says so
  on that server's row (#793).
- **Share to Linger from your phone** (#785, #820): Android opens Linger on
  your conversations; an iPhone sends from inside the share sheet.
- **Everybody in voice knows a share started** (#746): a sound, Watch in the
  voice bar, and a line in the chat saying who.
- **A drop of a minute or two in a busy call** no longer leaves you out of
  voice with a silent seat behind you (#678).
- **The voice strip shows as many people as fit** (#745), and a name on it
  opens that person's volume (#747).
- **A long message you send stays on screen whole** (#750, #760).
- **Bigger words on the phone**, with a Text size of its own (#690).

## Linger updates itself

- **On Windows (the setup.exe) and with the AppImage**, a day after a
  release is published, Linger downloads it and installs it at the next
  start, or after half an hour with nobody using it. Never while you're in
  voice or sharing your screen (#772).
- **With the .deb, .rpm and .msi**, which need a password to install, it
  downloads the update and asks, with **Update** at the foot of the list.
- **On Omarchy or Arch**, Linger still updates with your system.
- After an update, the foot of the list says **You're on Linger 0.5.x** once,
  with one line about what's new.
- **A host whose server is behind** sees it at the foot of the list, with
  **How to update**. The disk and backup lines there now say **Server
  settings** rather than Hosting….
- **A server says the oldest app it works with** (#793). An app older than
  that says so on that server's row and offers **Update now**, and its other
  servers keep working. Today that's 0.4.1, so nobody is turned away.

## Share to Linger from your phone

Linger is in the phone's share sheet, for pictures, videos and files (#785,
#792).

- **On Android**, sharing to Linger opens it on your conversations. Pick one,
  and what you shared waits in its message box, unsent, for you to add words
  and send.
- **On an iPhone**, Linger's own window rises inside the share sheet (#820).
  You see what you're sharing, can add a message, and pick a room, a group or
  a person. Your most recent ones are on top, and a search finds the rest.
  **Send** posts it there and then, and you're still in Photos (or wherever
  you shared from). With more than one server, each row says which server
  it's on.
- **The iPhone's share needs the server updated**: the window signs in to
  each server with a sign-in of its own, which only a 0.5.4 server gives. On
  an older server, its rooms show greyed out with "needs updating to share
  from here".
- **A photo the iPhone keeps as HEIC** arrives as a JPEG, at most 4,096
  pixels on its longest side (#817). The share window has too little memory
  to read a recent iPhone's photo whole, and doesn't need to.
- **Several pictures to a busy server**: one that has to wait its turn now
  goes up after all, or offers **Try again**, rather than leaving a red line
  you could only take out (#801).

## Screen sharing and voice

- **Everybody in voice knows a share started** (#746). They hear it start
  and stop, **Watch** stays lit while it goes on, the voice bar offers it,
  and a quiet line in the chat says who's sharing. That line needs the server
  updated.
- **The voice strip shows as many people as fit its line**, not a fixed four
  (#745).
- **A click on somebody on the conversation's voice line** opens their
  volume, as in the voice bar. On a phone it rises from the bottom, and the
  people there are easier to tap (#747).
- **An iPhone has no per-person volume any more** (#816). iOS doesn't let
  Linger set one voice's volume, so the slider moved and did nothing. There,
  a person's name in a call opens only the host's **Take out**. Android keeps
  its slider.
- **A short drop in a busy call** (#678). The server now keeps the last two
  minutes of what somebody missed while their connection was down, not only
  the last 500 things, so somebody back from a minute or two away is still
  in the call. When even that isn't enough, they start afresh at once and the
  call sees them leave then, rather than a silent seat staying for up to two
  more minutes.

## Conversations

- **A long message you send is on screen whole**, not left below the bottom
  with only your name showing, and "you left off here" no longer sits above
  it (#750).
- **A reaction or an edit on the newest message** keeps the conversation at
  its very end, not a few pixels short, and messages arriving in a burst no
  longer leave the newest below the bottom (#760).
- **A room's topic or a friend's status cut short with "…"** in the header
  opens whole with a click or a tap: in a card, or a sheet on a phone (#778).
- **On Linux with NVIDIA, a video in a conversation shows its picture again**,
  not solid green. That was a regression in 0.5.3 (#756).

## Phones and Settings

- **Bigger words on the phone**: Settings → Appearance has a **Text size**
  of its own, on top of the phone's. The iPhone app now follows the iPhone's
  Text Size, as the Android app follows its Font size (#690).
- **When storage won't keep an Appearance choice** (full, or switched off in
  a browser), Settings says "Couldn't save that here." and the setting stays
  as it was (#782). In the installed app it hardly ever happens.
- **Settings → Account & App** has **Privacy**, which opens the privacy page,
  and **Report a problem**, which opens GitHub's forms for a new issue
  (#779).
- **On an iPhone**, Linger says why it wants the local network when your
  server is on your home network (#779). It stays out of iCloud backups, and
  its sign-in stays on that phone (#790). Nobody is signed out by the update.
- **The iPhone app needs iOS 15 or later** (#743). Every iPhone that ran it
  before has it.

## Known issues

- **The iPhone's share window is new.** A TestFlight tester ran every check
  on a real iPhone, and it was tested on a simulated one against a real
  server. Tell us what it does with what you share most.
- **The Android share has no window of its own**: it opens Linger, as above.
- **Linger updates itself only from this release on.** 0.5.3 and older ask,
  as before.
- **A computer with NVIDIA's legacy driver can't watch a screen** (#729).

## Older apps and servers

Nothing stops working with an older app or server (PROTOCOL §9).

- **A 0.5.4 app on a 0.5.3 server** works as before, with every fix in the
  app. There's no line in the chat when somebody shares their screen, the
  iPhone's share window says that server needs updating, and a drop in a
  busy call is handled the old way.
- **A 0.5.3 app on a 0.5.4 server** works as before. It shows the share line
  as that person saying "shared their screen".

## For hosts: updating the server

Server image: `ghcr.io/itsmattguenther/linger:0.5.4` (x86-64 only), also
tagged `0.5` and `latest`.

### What changes for you

- **One small database change** (migration 0026): a column on messages for
  the share line, and an index for it. It takes a moment, even on a big
  server. `update.sh` saves the database into `backups/` first.
- **The iPhone's share window signs in** with `POST /auth/share-sign-in`,
  ten times an hour per person at most. Each is a sign-in like any other:
  changing a password, a reset or removing somebody ends it.
- **Somebody whose connection drops** is kept up to two minutes' worth of
  what they missed, in memory, so the server uses a little more memory in a
  busy call. Nothing to set.
- **No new setting**, and nothing in `compose.yaml` changes.

### The steps

On the server, in your `linger` folder:

```bash
cd ~/linger
./update.sh
```

(Use `sudo ./update.sh` if you type `sudo` before `docker` commands.) It
downloads 0.5.4 while the server keeps running, stops it for a few seconds to
save the database into `backups/`, then starts everything again and prints the
version before and after. If 0.5.4 doesn't start, it prints the commands that
put 0.5.3 back.

Check it worked, with your own address in place of `linger.example.com`:

```bash
curl -f https://linger.example.com/api/v1/health
```

It prints `{"ok":true,"version":"0.5.4","oldest_client":"0.4.1"}`.

**Going back to 0.5.3** means restoring the copy `update.sh` saved into
`backups/` just before 0.5.4 started, and anything posted since is lost. The
host guide's [Restoring](../host-guide.md#restoring) says how.

## Install or update

**This once, update the old way**: **Update…** at the foot of the list, or
Settings → Account & App → Install and restart. Windows users should keep the
same installer format (`.exe` or `.msi`) when upgrading. Linux packages are
`.AppImage`, `.deb` and `.rpm`; on Omarchy or Arch, Linger updates with your
system (Update in the Omarchy menu, or `sudo pacman -Syu`). Windows installers
are updater-signed, not Authenticode-signed; SmartScreen may still warn about
an unfamiliar app.

On Android, download `Linger_0.5.4_android-arm64.apk` from this release on
the phone and install it over the old one, or update from Google Play if
you're testing there. You stay signed in. On an iPhone, TestFlight offers
0.5.4 once it's through Apple's processing.

Record OS, package type, previous and new version, and what you shared, with
any failure. Use made-up pictures and messages.

   (AppImage) and one package that asks (`.deb`, `.rpm` or `.msi`): it
   installs, you stay signed in, and the foot of the list says **You're on
   Linger 0.5.4** once (#772).
2. **iPhone, through TestFlight**: share a photo from Photos to a room on a
   0.5.4 server, a minute after leaving Linger; it arrives, and you stay in
   Photos (#820, #825). Three photos at once arrive as one message. A room on
   an older server shows greyed out.
3. **Android**: share a picture to Linger; it waits in the conversation you
   pick (#785).
4. **A screen share in a call of three**: the others hear it start and stop,
   Watch stays lit, and a line in the chat says who's sharing (#746).
5. **Drop a phone's Wi-Fi for a minute in a busy call**, then bring it back:
   it's still in the call, with no silent seat left behind (#678).
6. **A long message you send** in a busy room stays on screen whole (#750).
7. **On Linux with NVIDIA**, a video in a conversation shows its picture
   (#756).
8. **The phone's Text size** in Settings → Appearance makes the words bigger,
   and the iPhone follows its own Text Size (#690).
