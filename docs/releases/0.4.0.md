# Linger 0.4.0: the buddy list

Linger has a new shape. The app is now one tall **buddy list** (you, the rooms
with who's in each, your DMs and everyone on the server), and conversations
open beside it, as tabs in one window or each in a window of its own. It keeps
running in the tray when you close it. Everything Linger did before, it still
does; much of it has moved, and this page says where.

Hosts: the server update is optional. It adds one thing (newcomers start on an
unused name color); the 0.4.0 app works with a 0.3.x server and the other way
round.

## The list

- **You** at the top: your name, where you are and your status (click it to
  change it), and **Away**, which remembers your recent away messages. With
  several servers, you choose which ones you're away on.
- **Rooms**, each with the dots of who's in it and a speaker when people are
  talking there. A room's name turns bold when something new is said, and
  never shows a number.
- **DMs**, named by who's in them. The pencil starts a new one, with one person
  or several.
- **People**: everyone on the server, with where they are and their status;
  **Away** and **Offline** fold up under them. Click someone for their card,
  with **Message** and **Knock**, or double-click them to go straight to a DM.
  A knock gives their row a little shake.
- **Several servers** are folding sections of the list, in your order, each
  with a **⋯** for **Quiet** (no sounds or banners from it) and moving it.
- At the foot, **Media** and **Search**. When you're in voice, the voice bar
  sits just above them.
- A quiet line at the foot, only while it's true, says when a server can't be
  reached, when this computer can't remember your sign-in, and when a new
  version of Linger is ready (**Update…**).

## Conversations

- Opening a room or a DM opens the **chat window**, with a tab for each
  conversation. **Ctrl+Tab** and **Ctrl+Shift+Tab** move between tabs,
  **Ctrl+W** closes one, and **Alt+1** to **Alt+9** jump to one. Your open tabs
  come back after a restart.
- The **⧉** button pops a conversation out into its own window, with what you
  were typing; **Back to tabs** puts it back. Or have every conversation open in
  its own window: **Settings → Windows**.
- Every window remembers its size and place, and draws its own title bar;
  tiling window managers tile them like any other window.
- Names are written inline, `Eli: …`, once per run of messages, and a reply's
  quote sits just above it. Click a name for that person's card.

## Voice

- Join from a room's tab: the line under its name says who's talking, with
  **Join** (or **Start talking**, or **Move voice here**).
- **Mute**, **Deafen** and **Leave** live in the voice bar at the bottom of your
  list, so they're always in reach. Closing a tab or window never ends voice.
- **Click someone in the voice bar to set how loud they are for you**, from
  silent to twice as loud. It's kept for that person, on this computer only.
- With the list tucked away in the tray, the tray menu has **Mute** and
  **Leave voice**.

## Search, Media and Settings

- **Search** and **Media** open in windows of their own, from the foot of the
  list. **Ctrl+K** opens Search from any window. A search hit or a media item
  opens its conversation at that message.
- The Media window says how long the server keeps files and how full it is.
- **Settings** is a window of its own too: the gear on the list, or **Ctrl+,**
  anywhere. New sections: **Windows** (tabs or windows, and what closing the
  list does), **Notifications** (desktop banners, split out of Sound & Voice)
  and **Servers** (with more than one: their order, Quiet, signing out of one).
- **Hosts:** the room, invite, member and server settings moved from the ⋯
  menu into **Settings → Hosting**.

## The tray, and one Linger at a time

- **Closing the list keeps Linger running in the tray**, with voice, knocks and
  notifications. **Show Linger** in the tray menu (or a click on the icon, on
  Windows) brings the list back, and **Quit Linger** quits. Prefer closing to
  quit? **Settings → Windows → When You Close Your List.**
- Starting Linger again while it's running brings up the running copy's list,
  rather than a second Linger.
- On Linux the tray needs a tray area (Waybar's tray module, or an extension
  on GNOME) and the `libayatana-appindicator` library, which the packages now
  recommend. Without them, closing the list quits, as before.

## Signing in

- Signed in nowhere, the list opens on one box: paste an invite, a server's
  address, or a setup link. Sign-ins from earlier versions carry over.
- **Add a server** is in **Settings → Account & App** (or **Servers**, with more
  than one).

## The look

- Night blue, with lamp amber for the few things that matter, from the porch
  in Linger's logo. Linger's small mark is now the porch door with the light
  on, in the title bar and the tray.
- **Dark only for now.** The light theme and evening warmth come back once the
  new look has versions of its own.
- The status bar is gone. Its jobs moved to the list's foot (connections,
  sign-ins, updates) and to the Media window (storage).

## On the server

- **A newcomer's name starts in a color**: the one the fewest people on the
  server wear, instead of gray, so a big group isn't a list of gray names.
  Anyone can change theirs in Settings → Profile. This needs the 0.4.0 server.

## The previous look, for one release

If the new list gets in your way, the previous client is still inside 0.4.0:
start Linger with the environment variable `LINGER_CLASSIC=1` (on Linux,
`LINGER_CLASSIC=1 linger-client`). It shares your sign-ins and servers. It
goes away in the next release, so tell us what you missed:
[open an issue](https://github.com/itsMattGuenther/Linger/issues/new/choose).

## Install or update

Use the in-app update: **Update…** at the foot of the list, or Settings →
Account & App → Updates. Windows users should keep the same installer format
(`.exe` or `.msi`) when upgrading. Linux packages are `.AppImage`, `.deb` and
`.rpm`; on Omarchy or Arch, Linger updates with your system. Windows
installers are updater-signed, not Authenticode-signed; SmartScreen may still
warn about an unfamiliar app.

Server image: `ghcr.io/itsmattguenther/linger:0.4.0` (amd64 and arm64). To
update: `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d` (add `--profile voice`
to both if you run the voice relay). No database changes, no new settings,
and no protocol changes; older apps keep working.

## Checks on installed clients

Record OS, package type, previous and new version, and interface size with
any failure. Use made-up messages, and keep private conversations out of
screenshots.

1. Update from 0.3.6. Linger opens on the list, still signed in, with your
   servers.
2. Close the list: a door icon appears in the tray, and voice keeps going.
   **Show Linger** brings the list back; **Quit Linger** quits. In **Settings →
   Windows**, switch closing to quit, and check that closing the list now quits.
3. Start Linger while it's running: the running list comes forward, and no
   second copy starts.
4. Open two rooms and a DM: one chat window, three tabs. Pop one out and put it
   back. Restart Linger: the tabs come back.
5. Join voice with somebody on another computer. Mute, deafen and leave from
   the voice bar; set their volume from their name; with the list in the tray,
   mute and leave from the tray menu.
6. Send a file by dragging it onto the message box (Windows especially), and
   download one.
7. **Ctrl+K** opens Search; a hit opens its conversation at the message. Media
   opens and stars a file.
8. Settings → Appearance → Interface Size at 150% and 200%: every window grows,
   and nothing is cut off.
9. Knock somebody, and have them knock you.

Voice with four people on four networks for an hour is still to be tried
(#197).
