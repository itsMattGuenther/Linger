# Linger 0.4.7: headsets and voice messages

Gaming headsets on Windows now work in voice: no more joining and not being
heard. You can record a voice message in any conversation, hear it back, and
send it or throw it away. And a few things got easier to notice: still being
away, which picture you're about to send, and where Linger went when you
closed its list.

**Hosts: update the server so people can send voice messages.** There's no
database change and no new setting. 0.4.7 apps work with a 0.4.6 server,
except that a voice message can't be sent until the server is updated.
0.4.6 apps work with a 0.4.7 server, and play voice messages as ordinary
audio files.
[For hosts](#for-hosts-updating-the-server) has the steps.

**Everyone: update the app.** The headset fix is in the app, so it reaches
you when you update, whatever your server runs.

## The highlights

- **Gaming headsets work in voice on Windows** (#398). A Logitech PRO X 2 set
  to 7.1 surround couldn't join voice at all, and a SteelSeries Arctis Nova 5
  joined but nobody could hear it. Linger now opens every microphone and
  speaker in the format it already runs in, and turns that into voice itself.
  If you set your headset to "Stereo, 16 bit, 48000 Hz" in Control Panel to
  get around this, you can set it back.
- **Voice messages** (#401). Press the microphone beside **+** in the message
  box, then **Record** and **Stop**. Listen back, then **Send voice message**
  or **Discard**. Up to five minutes.
- **Still being away is hard to miss** (#392). While you're away, the top of
  your list turns a warm cream with a filled **I'm back**, and when you come
  back to your computer, it says "Welcome back. You're still away."

## Voice

- **Headsets in their own format** (#398). On Windows, Linger used to ask every
  headset for 16-bit stereo sound at 48 kHz, and some refused: surround-sound
  headsets, 24-bit ones, and ones running through SteelSeries Sonar. Now it
  takes whatever the headset gives. Nothing changes on Linux or for
  headsets that already worked.
- **Voice messages** (#401).
  - Record and Stop are presses, never a hold, so you can let go of the mouse
    while you talk. Lines move with your voice, and the time counts up.
  - Stopping never sends anything. You hear it back first.
  - At five minutes it stops by itself and keeps what you have.
  - It records from the microphone picked in Settings → Sound & Voice, or your
    computer's default.
  - In the conversation it shows as **Voice message** with a play button and
    its length, and it plays on Windows and Linux alike.
  - **Voice rooms are still never recorded.** A voice message is a clip you
    record of yourself and choose to send, like sharing an audio file.
  - The desktop app records them. The phone app will later.
- **Why voice didn't start, in full** (#399). When voice fails to start, the
  short reason above the conversation is now a button. Click it and the whole
  reason opens and stays open, including your computer's own words for what
  failed, with a **Copy** button for sending it to your host.

## Your status

- **Away looks like away** (#392). While you're away:
  - Your card at the top of the list is tinted a warm cream, not the amber of
    something new.
  - Your away message sits in a box with the moon, where your status was.
  - **I'm back** is filled in the same cream.
  - Settings → Profile's "You're away" box matches.
- **Coming back says so** (#392). When you come back to your computer after
  ten minutes or more and you're still away, the card says "Welcome back.
  You're still away." **I'm back** is right there, or **✕** to stay away.
  Linger never sets you back by itself.

## Sharing files

- **A picture shows a small preview before you send it** (#397). A phone
  photo is called something like `9D836261-01DD-….jpg`, so the name alone
  didn't say which one you'd picked. Now a picture's line above the box starts
  with a small square of it, made on your computer. Other files keep the file
  icon.

## Closing the list

- **Linger says where it went** (#400). Closing the list keeps Linger running
  in the tray, with voice, as before. The first time you do it on a computer,
  a notification says Linger is still running, where its icon is, and that
  you're still in voice if you are. It says it once, and never again.
- **On Windows 11 the tray icon starts out hidden** under the **^** by the
  clock. Windows doesn't let an app show its own icon, but you can: Settings →
  Personalization → Taskbar → **Other system tray icons** → **Linger**. The
  [user guide](../user-guide.md) has this too.

## Under the hood

- Two browser tests that failed at random on a busy machine are steady
  (#394, #396). This changes only the checks.

## Known issues

- **On Hyprland (Omarchy included), the mouse pointer jumps into a
  conversation that opens in a window of its own** (popped out, or with
  **Each in its own window**). Conversations beside the list don't do this.
  It's Hyprland's behaviour when an app brings a window forward, and the
  [user guide](../user-guide.md#worth-knowing) has the two lines that turn it
  off.
- **The AppImage doesn't play AV1 videos**, which some NVIDIA and OBS game
  recordings and Pixel 10 phones make. Its own video decoders cover H.264 and
  H.265, which almost every phone and recorder uses (#358). Windows and the
  Arch, `.deb` and `.rpm` packages play AV1.

## For hosts: updating the server

Server image: `ghcr.io/itsmattguenther/linger:0.4.7` (x86-64 only), also
tagged `latest`.

**What changes for you:**

- **Voice messages are accepted** (#401). They're ordinary audio files, about
  half a megabyte a minute, and count against your storage limit like any
  other file. The server doesn't convert them, so there's nothing to install.
- **No database change and no new setting.** The image is still x86-64 only.
  ARM servers stay on 0.4.4.
- **Nothing else** about voice changes on the server. The headset fix is in
  the app.

Run these **on the server**, one block at a time.

**1. Go to the folder with your `compose.yaml`** (the `linger` folder from
setup):

```bash
cd ~/linger
```

**2. Update:**

```bash
./update.sh
```

(Use `sudo ./update.sh` if you type `sudo` before `docker` commands. No
`update.sh` in the folder? The [0.4.5 notes](0.4.5.md#for-hosts-updating-the-server)
show how to get it.) It downloads 0.4.7 while the server keeps running, stops
it for a few seconds to save the database into `backups/`, starts everything
again, and prints the version before and after.

**3. Check it worked:**

```bash
curl -f https://linger.example.com/api/v1/health
```

With your own address in place of `linger.example.com`, it should print
`{"ok":true,"version":"0.4.7"}`.

**If something goes wrong**, `update.sh` prints the commands that put 0.4.6
back. With no database change, going back is safe. Voice messages sent in the
meantime stay, but a 0.4.6 server offers them as downloads instead of
playing them, until you update again.

## Install or update

Use the in-app update: **Update…** at the foot of the list, or Settings →
Account & App → Updates. Windows users should keep the same installer format
(`.exe` or `.msi`) when upgrading. Linux packages are `.AppImage`, `.deb` and
`.rpm`; on Omarchy or Arch, Linger updates with your system. Windows
installers are updater-signed, not Authenticode-signed; SmartScreen may still
warn about an unfamiliar app.

## Checks on installed clients

Record OS, package type, previous and new version, and headset or microphone
with any failure. Use made-up messages, and keep private conversations out of
screenshots.

1. **Windows, with a gaming headset** (a Logitech PRO X 2 in 7.1, a
   SteelSeries Arctis Nova 5, or SteelSeries Sonar on): join voice. Your name
   lights up when you talk, others hear you, and you hear them (#398).
2. **Windows, a headset set to Stereo, 16 bit, 48000 Hz** that already
   worked: it still does (#398).
3. **After the host updates the server:** record a voice message, stop, play
   it back, and send it. A friend on Windows and one on Linux both play it.
   Discard one instead: nothing is sent (#401).
4. Close the list with ✕ for the first time on a computer: a notification says
   where Linger went, and mentions voice if you're in it. Close it again:
   nothing (#400).
5. Press **Away**: your card turns cream with a filled **I'm back**. Leave the
   computer for over ten minutes, then move the mouse over Linger: "Welcome
   back. You're still away." (#392).
6. Attach a phone photo: a small preview shows above the box (#397).
7. **Windows**, with the microphone privacy switch off: try to join voice,
   click the reason, and **Copy** it (#399).
