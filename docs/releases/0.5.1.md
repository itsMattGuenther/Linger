# Linger 0.5.1: voice on your phone, deleting your account, and devices that agree

The Android app joins voice rooms now, and a locked phone stays in the call.
Anybody can delete their own account from inside the app. Your computer and
your phone stop contradicting each other: reading on one clears the other,
and what one says about where you are no longer overwrites the other. Voice
recovers in seconds when the network changes. Shared videos lose the place
they were filmed. And on Linux, a long sleep no longer leaves Linger stuck
signing back in, and dropping a file on the message box works again.

**Everyone: update the app.** Phone voice, the voice fixes and the Linux fixes
are in the app.

**Hosts: update the server** with `./update.sh`, as usual. **It changes the
database** (one change, `0025`), so going back to 0.5.0 means restoring the
copy `update.sh` makes. Nothing in `compose.yaml` or `.env` changes. Deleting
an account, reading on one device clearing the others, and two devices
showing as one all need the server updated. See
[For hosts](#for-hosts-updating-the-server).

## The highlights

- **Voice rooms on the Android app** (#429). Join from a room, talk with the
  phone's microphone or a headset, and lock the phone without leaving the
  call.
- **Delete your own account** (#650), from either app, and choose whether
  what you wrote goes with it.
- **Read on one device, and it's read on the others** (#647), even the ones
  that stayed open.
- **One person on two devices is shown as one** (#653), and a short network
  drop no longer shows you offline (#654).
- **Voice comes back in seconds after a network change** (#656), and a lost
  packet is rebuilt rather than guessed at (#655).
- **Videos and sound files keep no trace of where they were made** (#651).
- **Linux:** no more "Signing you back in…" for a quarter of an hour after a
  long sleep (#646), and dropping a file on the message box attaches it again
  (#673).

## Voice on your phone

- **Join from a room** (#429), the same way as on a computer: the line under
  the room's name says who's talking, with **Join**. Android asks for the
  microphone the first time, unless it already did for a voice message.
- **Mute, Deafen and Leave are a thumb's size**, in the room's line and in the
  voice bar at the bottom of the list.
- **Your voice follows you.** A conversation, Media or Search covers the list
  on a phone, so every other screen shows one line under its title, "In voice
  in #general", with your controls. Tap its words to go back to the room. In
  another room it takes the place of that room's own line: to talk there
  instead, leave, then join.
- **Lock the phone and stay in the call.** While you're in voice, Android
  shows one silent bar, "In voice in #general", with **Mute** and **Leave**.
  It's the only notification Linger ever shows: it makes no sound, says
  nothing about messages, and goes when you leave. The first time you join,
  Android asks whether Linger may send notifications. That's for this bar.
  Say no and the call still keeps going with the phone locked; you just
  don't get the bar.
- **The phone does the cleaning up.** Phone voice uses the phone's own echo
  cancelling and noise suppression, so a phone on speaker doesn't send the
  room back to itself. Sound comes out of the speaker, or a headset when one
  is connected.
- **No push-to-talk on a phone**: Mute is how you go quiet. A person's volume
  goes up to 100% on a phone, not 200%, because the phone's player goes no
  louder than as sent.
- Phone voice needs no server update, and people on computers hear a phone
  like anybody else. The iPhone gets voice with the rest of the iPhone app.

## Your account

- **Delete your own account** (#650): Settings → Account & App → **Delete
  Your Account**. It asks for your password, offers to download your copy
  first, and has one box: **Also delete everything I wrote**, off to start.
  - **Box off:** your messages, files, reactions and poll votes stay, without
    your name. They say **Deleted account**.
  - **Box on:** they go, the way a deleted message does. A closed poll keeps
    how it came out.
  - Either way your profile, status, settings and sign-ins go at once, and
    nobody new can take your username, so nobody inherits your old mentions.
    Group DMs say you left; a one-to-one DM stays your friend's.
- **The host can't delete their own account.** Settings says why.
- **The host can delete a removed person's account**: Settings → Hosting →
  People → Removed → **Delete account**, with the same box. Only the host,
  not a co-host.
- On a server that hasn't updated, Settings says it needs an update instead of
  offering a delete it would refuse.

## Two devices at once

- **Reading on one device clears it on the others** (#647). Read on your phone
  and the desktop left open stops showing those rooms as new, and the other
  way round. Only your own devices are told, and only where you've read to,
  never a count. This needs both the app and the server updated.
- **One person on two devices is shown as one** (#653). Before, whichever
  device spoke last was what everybody saw: idle while you typed on your
  phone, or around after the phone closed. Now away on any device is away; in
  a room on any device is in that room; and you're idle only when every
  device is. **I'm back** on any device ends away everywhere.
- **A short network drop no longer shows you offline** (#654), the phone
  moving from Wi-Fi to mobile data, say. Nobody sees anything unless you're
  gone for more than 5 seconds, and your away message stays.

## Voice

- **Back in seconds after the network changes** (#656). When the network
  under a call changed, the room went quiet for about half a minute. Now the
  app asks the server for a fresh connection about 5 seconds after it stops
  hearing anything, and straight away when its own connection comes back.
- **A lost packet is rebuilt, not guessed at** (#655). Each voice packet has
  always carried a rougher copy of the one before. The app now uses that copy
  to rebuild a lost one, so a patchy line sounds closer to the real thing.
  It works with friends on any version.

## Files

- **Videos and sound files keep no trace of where they were made** (#651). A
  phone writes the place a video was filmed inside the file. The server now
  copies every video and sound file into a fresh one without its tags, the
  way it already stripped photos. Nothing is re-encoded, so it's quick, and a
  portrait video stays upright. Shared songs lose their title, artist and
  cover picture too.
- **A portrait phone video is the right shape** (#658): the server describes
  it as taller than wide, the way it plays.
- Both are on the server, and only for files shared from now on.

## Linux

- **No more "Signing you back in…" after a long sleep** (#646). After an
  hour or more asleep, Linger sat on it for about 15 minutes. The window's
  network connection to the server had gone dead in the sleep, and every
  try went down it. Linger now makes a fresh connection for each try.
- **Dropping a file on the message box attaches it again** (#673). A recent
  WebKitGTK (the engine Linger draws with on Linux) hands a page the files
  dropped on it as nothing. Linger now takes the drop itself and gives the
  message box exactly the files you dropped. A folder says to drop the files
  in it instead, and anything over 500 MB says it's too big. Windows is
  unchanged.
- **No sideways scroll bar under an empty message box** (#672).

## Fixes

- **Emoji shortcodes work while editing a message** (#666): `:smiley:` and the
  list as you type, as in the message box.

## Under the hood

- **The signing keys live in a locked-down place** (#652): only a release
  from `main` can reach them. Nothing changes for you.
- **The website is [linger.im](https://linger.im)**, and Windows' Apps &
  features and the Linux packages link to it.
- **Which apps and servers work together** is written down (#315, PROTOCOL
  §9): until 1.0, every change works both ways wherever it can.

## Known issues

- **Quitting Linger shows you offline about 5 seconds late** (#654): Quit
  doesn't say goodbye yet, so the server waits out a short drop first.
- **Files shared before this release keep their tags**, and portrait videos
  shared before it keep their sideways size.
- **Phone voice is new.** It was tested on Android emulators against a real
  server and in browser tests against a real voice connection, and a 5-person
  call has worked, but tell us how it sounds and how the call bar behaves on
  your phone.

## Older apps and servers

Nothing stops working with an older app or server (PROTOCOL §9).

- **A 0.5.0 app on a 0.5.1 server** works as before. It shows a deleted
  account's messages as "someone", and doesn't hear reading on your other
  devices until it reconnects.
- **A 0.5.1 app on an older server** works too. Deleting an account says the
  server needs an update, reading on one device clears the others only after
  a reconnect, and two devices are shown as before. Phone voice works with
  any server from 0.4.1.

## For hosts: updating the server

Server image: `ghcr.io/itsmattguenther/linger:0.5.1` (x86-64 only), also
tagged `0.5` and `latest`.

### What changes for you

- **The database changes once** (`0025`, deleted accounts). It runs by itself
  the first time 0.5.1 starts. `update.sh` saves the database just before, and
  that copy is how you'd go back: 0.5.0 won't start on a database 0.5.1 has
  changed.
- **Videos and sound files need ffmpeg** (#651). The published image has it,
  so nothing changes if you run the image. A server built from source without
  ffmpeg now refuses videos and sound files, and says the host needs to
  install it.
- **Nothing in `compose.yaml` or `.env` changes**, and there are no new
  settings.
- **What needs the server updated, not just the app:** deleting an account,
  reading on one device clearing the others, two devices shown as one, a short
  drop not showing offline, and videos losing their location.

### The steps

On the server, in your `linger` folder:

```bash
cd ~/linger
./update.sh
```

(Use `sudo ./update.sh` if you type `sudo` before `docker` commands.) It
downloads 0.5.1 while the server keeps running, stops it for a few seconds to
save the database into `backups/`, then starts everything again and prints the
version before and after. If 0.5.1 doesn't start, it prints the commands that
put 0.5.0 back.

Check it worked, with your own address in place of `linger.example.com`:

```bash
curl -f https://linger.example.com/api/v1/health
```

It prints `{"ok":true,"version":"0.5.1"}`.

**Going back to 0.5.0** means restoring the copy `update.sh` saved into
`backups/` just before 0.5.1 started, and anything posted since is lost. The
[0.5.0 notes](0.5.0.md#for-hosts-updating-the-server) and the host guide's
[Restoring](../host-guide.md#restoring) say how.

## Install or update

Use the in-app update: **Update…** at the foot of the list, or Settings →
Account & App → Updates. Windows users should keep the same installer format
(`.exe` or `.msi`) when upgrading. Linux packages are `.AppImage`, `.deb` and
`.rpm`; on Omarchy or Arch, Linger updates with your system (Update in the
Omarchy menu, or `sudo pacman -Syu`). Windows installers are updater-signed,
not Authenticode-signed; SmartScreen may still warn about an unfamiliar app.

On Android, download `Linger_0.5.1_android-arm64.apk` from this release on
the phone and install it over the old one. You stay signed in. A first install
is step by step in the [0.4.8 notes](0.4.8.md#the-phone-app).

## Checks on installed clients

Record OS, package type, previous and new version, and headset or microphone
with any failure. Use made-up messages, and keep private conversations out of
screenshots.

1. **Phone voice** on an Android phone, with somebody on a computer in the
   room: join, talk both ways, on speaker and with a headset (#429).
2. **The call bar:** lock the phone for ten minutes in voice, then use the
   bar's **Mute** and **Leave** (#429).
3. **A network change mid-call**, on a computer (Wi-Fi to wired or a phone
   hotspot) and on the phone (Wi-Fi to mobile data): the voice comes back
   within seconds (#656).
4. **Delete an account** on a test account, once with the box off and once
   on, and as the host for somebody removed (#650).
5. **Two devices:** read on the phone with the desktop open, and the room
   stops looking new there (#647). Go away on one, open a room on the other:
   still away (#653).
6. **Linux after a long sleep:** sleep an hour or more, wake: signed in within
   seconds (#646).
7. **Drop a file on the message box** on Omarchy, from the file manager: it
   attaches. A folder says to drop the files in it (#673).
8. **A video filmed on a phone** with location on: download it from Linger and
   check it has no location (`exiftool`), and a portrait one plays upright
   (#651, #658).
9. **Windows:** Apps & features shows linger.im for Linger.
