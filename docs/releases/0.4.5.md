# Linger 0.4.5: one window

Your list and your conversations now live in one window. Each friend has one
row: click it, and they open beside the list, their card on top and your
conversation underneath. Long messages use the whole width of a wide window,
and shared videos play with sound on Linux.

**Hosts: updating is quick, and optional this time.** The server hasn't
changed apart from its version number: no database change and no new
setting. 0.4.5 apps work with a 0.4.4 server. Your app will now tell you the
server is behind, though, so get the new update script and run it. **On an
ARM server, such as a Raspberry Pi, stay on 0.4.4.**
[For hosts](#for-hosts-updating-the-server) has the steps.

**Everyone: update the app.**

## The highlights

- **One window.** Conversations, Media and Search open beside your list as
  tabs, and fold back to just the list (#337).
- **One row per friend.** Your DM with somebody lives on their row. Click it
  and they open beside the list (#351).
- **Long messages use the whole width** of a wide window (#334).
- **Shared videos play with sound on Linux** (#358).
- **Hosts update with one command**, and the app says when the server is
  behind (#312, #314).

## Your list and conversations

- **The list is the one window** (#337). Open a room or a person and the
  window grows to the right to make room for it (to the left if your screen
  ends there). The list and the conversation move, snap and minimize
  together.
  - Each conversation gets a tab. **Ctrl+Tab** moves between them and
    **Ctrl+W** closes one.
  - The fold button at the conversations' left edge folds the window back to
    just your list and keeps your tabs. The button beside the gear brings them
    back. Closing the last tab folds it too.
  - Drag the line between the list and the conversations to make the list
    wider or narrower. Double-click it to put it back.
  - A maximized or snapped window keeps its size. On a tiling desktop like
    Hyprland (Omarchy included), the window keeps its tile. If the tile is too
    narrow for both, the conversation takes the whole window and the fold
    button takes you back to the list.
  - **Media and Search** open as tabs too, and **Ctrl+K** opens Search. A
    result opens its conversation as another tab, at the message.
  - The pop-out button still puts a tab in a window of its own, and **Back
    beside your list** puts it back. If you'd rather every conversation had
    its own window, choose that in **Settings → Windows**.
  - The separate chat window, with its own tabs, is gone.
- **One row per friend** (#351). Every friend used to be in the list twice,
  once under DMs and once under People. Now there's no DMs section: your DM
  with somebody lives on their row in **People**.
  - Their row **lights up** when they've written something you haven't read,
    even when **Away** or **Offline** is folded.
  - **The people you're talking to come first** in each group: whoever wrote
    to you, then whoever you talked with most recently, then everyone else.
  - **Click a person** and they open beside the list: their name, where they
    are, their whole status and fields, and **Knock** on top, your
    conversation underneath.
  - That tab is a **preview**, its name in italics. Clicking the next person
    replaces it, so looking around doesn't pile up tabs. Typing in it keeps
    it.
  - **Hover** a person to see their whole status when the row had to cut it
    short. The list marks whatever is showing beside it.
  - **Group DMs sit with the rooms**, after the server's own. The **+** on
    the **Rooms** heading starts one.
  - The small **card button** on a person's row, next to Knock, opens just
    their card, without opening a tab.
- **Long messages use the whole width** (#334). A message's words used to
  stop at 80 characters, however wide the window. Now they run up to the time
  column. At a normal window size nothing changes. Search results keep the
  shorter lines.

## Fixes

- **Linux: shared videos play with sound** (#358). Most videos from a phone
  or a screen recorder have AAC sound and an H.264 picture. Linux's media
  system needs its libav decoders for both, and Linger never asked for them,
  so those videos played silent or not at all.
  - The **Arch and Omarchy package** requires them now.
  - A **fresh install of the `.deb` or `.rpm`** brings them.
  - The **AppImage** carries its own, trimmed to what shared videos need. It
    grew 1.4 MB. It doesn't play AV1, or older formats such as DivX.
  - **Updating a `.deb` or `.rpm` from inside the app doesn't add them**
    (#377). Add them once with the command under
    [Install or update](#install-or-update).
- **Linux: the desktop's play key can't start a song you paused** (#353).
  The play/pause key, or a mouse button the desktop treats as one, could
  start a song posted in a room even though you'd paused it. Now Play only
  resumes something the desktop itself paused. Pause and play in the middle
  of a song work as before.
- **Names in Silkscreen show in Silkscreen** (#318). In installed apps, a
  name set in Silkscreen, and the small logo mark, fell back to another font.
  It never worked before this release.
- **No yellow ring after switching workspaces** (#375). Coming back to Linger
  could leave a focus ring around whatever you'd last clicked. The ring now
  shows only after you've used the keyboard.
- **End on a shared audio file's timeline goes to the end** (#343). On Linux
  it could turn the player into "Couldn't load this audio."

## Known issues

- **On Hyprland (Omarchy included), the mouse pointer jumps into a
  conversation that opens in a window of its own** (popped out, or with
  **Each in its own window**). Conversations beside the list don't do this.
  It's Hyprland's behaviour when an app brings a window forward, and the
  [user guide](../user-guide.md#worth-knowing) has the two lines that turn it
  off.

## For hosts: updating the server

Server image: `ghcr.io/itsmattguenther/linger:0.4.5` (x86-64 only), also
tagged `latest`.

**What changes for you:**

- **Nothing in the server itself.** No database change and no new setting.
  0.4.5 apps work with a 0.4.4 server.
- **Your app says when the server is behind** (#314). **Settings → Hosting →
  Server** shows the version your server runs. When a newer one is out, it
  says so, with **How to update** and **What's new**. Only the host sees it.
- **One command updates the server** (#312): `./update.sh`. It backs up the
  database first, and keeps the last five backups.
- **The image is for x86-64 (Intel or AMD) only** from this release on
  (#310). ARM servers stay on 0.4.4.
- **If you run the voice relay**, `.env.example` now has
  `COMPOSE_PROFILES=voice`. Put that line in your `.env` and a plain
  `docker compose up -d` starts the relay too. It's optional, since
  `update.sh` works it out either way.

Run these **on the server**, one block at a time.

**1. Go to the folder with your `compose.yaml`** (the `linger` folder from
setup):

```bash
cd ~/linger
```

**2. Check what your server runs on:**

```bash
uname -m
```

`x86_64` means carry on. `aarch64` or `arm64` means an ARM server: **stop
here.** Change the image line to `ghcr.io/itsmattguenther/linger:0.4.4` with
`nano compose.yaml`, and leave it there. Otherwise `latest` points at an
image your server can't run, and every update fails with "no matching
manifest". Your server is missing nothing that 0.4.5 apps need.

**3. Get the update script.** Once only; servers set up from 0.4.5 on already
have it:

```bash
curl -fLO https://raw.githubusercontent.com/itsMattGuenther/Linger/main/deploy/update.sh
chmod +x update.sh
```

**4. Update:**

```bash
./update.sh
```

(Use `sudo ./update.sh` if you type `sudo` before `docker` commands.) It
downloads 0.4.5 while the server keeps running, stops it for a few seconds to
save the database into `backups/`, starts everything again, the relay too,
and prints the version before and after. If it stops because the image line
still says `matthewguenther`, change that line to
`ghcr.io/itsmattguenther/linger:latest` with `nano compose.yaml` and run it
again.

**5. Check it worked:**

```bash
curl -f https://linger.example.com/api/v1/health
```

With your own address in place of `linger.example.com`, it should print
`{"ok":true,"version":"0.4.5"}`. **Settings → Hosting → Server** in your app
should now say it's the newest.

**If something goes wrong**, `update.sh` prints the commands that put 0.4.4
back. The database didn't change, so going back is only the image line:
change it to `ghcr.io/itsmattguenther/linger:0.4.4` and run
`docker compose up -d`.

## Install or update

Use the in-app update: **Update…** at the foot of the list, or Settings →
Account & App → Updates. Windows users should keep the same installer format
(`.exe` or `.msi`) when upgrading. Linux packages are `.AppImage`, `.deb` and
`.rpm`; on Omarchy or Arch, Linger updates with your system. Windows
installers are updater-signed, not Authenticode-signed; SmartScreen may still
warn about an unfamiliar app.

**Linux, with a `.deb` or `.rpm` you updated from inside the app:** add the
video decoders once, then restart Linger.

- Debian and Ubuntu: `sudo apt install gstreamer1.0-libav`
- Fedora: `sudo dnf install gstreamer1-plugin-libav`

## Checks on installed clients

Record OS, package type, previous and new version, and interface size with
any failure. Use made-up messages, and keep private conversations out of
screenshots.

1. **Windows 11:** with the list near the right edge of the screen, open a
   room: the window grows to the left. Snap the window to half the screen and
   open a room: it keeps its size (#337).
2. **Hyprland:** open a room with the window tiled, then floating. Tiled, it
   keeps its tile, and in a narrow tile the conversation takes the whole
   window. Floating, it grows beside the list (#337).
3. Fold with the fold button, bring the tabs back with the button beside the
   gear, and close the last tab: it folds. **Ctrl+K** opens Search as a tab,
   and a result opens its conversation at the message.
4. Click a friend: their card on top, your conversation below, the tab in
   italics. Click another friend: that tab is replaced. Type in it: it stays
   (#351).
5. Have a friend DM you while their group is folded: their row and the
   group's heading light up. Read it: the light goes out (#351).
6. Start a group with the **+** on **Rooms**: it sits after the server's
   rooms.
7. In a window over 1600 pixels wide, send a long message: its words run to
   just short of the time (#334).
8. **Linux:** play an MP4 from a phone in a room: it has sound. Try it on the
   Arch package, on an AppImage, and on a `.deb` or `.rpm` updated in-app
   after adding the decoders (#358).
9. **Debian or Ubuntu**, on a computer without `gstreamer1.0-libav`: update
   from 0.4.4 inside the app. It finishes, Linger restarts on 0.4.5, and
   `sudo apt install` of anything still works afterwards (#377).
10. **Linux:** leave a song paused in a room and press the play key (or a
    mouse button bound to it) a few times: it stays paused. Play a song,
    press pause, then play: it pauses and carries on (#353).
11. Set your name in Silkscreen: it shows in Silkscreen, on Linux and on
    Windows (#318).
12. Click a tab, switch to another workspace and back: no yellow ring. Press
    Tab: the ring shows (#375).
13. **Linux:** share an audio file, click its timeline and press End: it goes
    to the end, and doesn't say it couldn't load (#343).
14. **As a host**, with the server still on 0.4.4: **Settings → Hosting →
    Server** says 0.4.5 is out, and **How to update** opens the host guide.
    Run `./update.sh`: it backs up, updates and prints 0.4.5. Settings then
    says it's the newest (#312, #314).
