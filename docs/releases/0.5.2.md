# Linger 0.5.2: share your screen

Anybody in a room's voice can share their screen now, and everybody else in
that voice can watch: slides, a document, some code. It works from the
Windows and Linux apps, at 30 or 60 frames a second, and you can watch on a
computer or a phone. With several servers, the list now opens with every
server folded, so none of them hides the others, and Settings changes who you
are on each one. And the iPhone app picks up the fixes its first testers asked
for.

**Everyone: update the app.** Sharing and watching are in the app.

**Hosts: update the server** with `./update.sh`, as usual. Screen sharing
needs it. There's no database change, and nothing in `compose.yaml` changes.
See [For hosts](#for-hosts-updating-the-server).

## The highlights

- **Share your screen** from Windows or Linux, and watch from every app (#360).
- **The PowerPoint trap is caught** (#722): a share that goes black, the
  slide show in a window of its own, says so.
- **A different you on each server, for real now** (#719), and every server
  starts folded (#712).
- **The away picker is yours** (#708): a bank of six you build.
- **The iPhone app's first fixes**, from its TestFlight testers.

## Share your screen

- **Share** sits beside Mute, Deafen and Leave when you're in a room's voice
  (#360). Pick **Sharp text, 30 fps** for slides, documents and code, or
  **Smooth motion, 60 fps** for scrolling, video or a game. Your computer's
  own window asks what to share: the whole screen or one window. Linger never
  sees that list or the names in it; everybody sees "Jules is sharing their
  screen", never what it is.
- **Watch** is on the room's voice line. It joins that room's voice first if
  you weren't in it, and the screen opens above the conversation, which
  carries on underneath. Drag the line between them, or press **Full
  screen**, or **F**, or double-click the picture. On a phone the screen sits
  at the top of the room, and turning the phone sideways fills the screen
  with it.
- **Your share in the voice bar**: a copy of what everybody sees, who is
  watching, by name, and **Stop**. The eye beside Stop hides the copy and
  keeps it hidden next time; hidden, your computer doesn't make it at all.
  Share from a conversation popped out into its own window, and that window
  shows all of it too, under its voice line. Stop is in the tray menu too,
  for when a slide show covers everything.
- **Up to three people** can share in a room at once. You watch one at a
  time.
- **Your connection slow?** The screen you're watching says so ("Your
  connection is slow, so Jules's screen is behind") until it catches up.
  Only you see it: the person sharing and everybody else watching are fine.
  A connection that drops without a word is noticed within about fifteen
  seconds, and Linger connects again by itself.
- **A phone stops watching** after half a minute out of sight, so it isn't
  using your data for a screen nobody sees.
- **Stopped to pick another window?** When somebody stops sharing, their
  screen stays open saying so, and if they share again it opens there by
  itself. Nobody has to press Watch again. Close it and it stays closed.
- **Pictures go up to 1080p.** A bigger screen, a 4K one, is sent at 1080p,
  its shape kept. Small text reads better if you share one window rather
  than a whole 4K screen.
- **Showing a PowerPoint?** Share **Entire screen**, then start the slide
  show. Sharing only the PowerPoint window can come out black, because the
  slide show runs in a window of its own. If your share stays black for a
  few seconds, Linger says so under it until you stop, and once as a desktop
  notice (#722). Windows may hold the notice back while a slide show fills
  the screen, so the people watching may tell you first.
- **Two monitors?** PowerPoint's Presenter View puts your notes on one
  monitor and the slides on the other. Share the monitor with the slides:
  under **Entire screen**, the picker shows each one. Picked your notes?
  Presenter View's **Display Settings** → **Swap Presenter View and Slide
  Show** fixes it without sharing again.
- **No sound from the computer yet**, and nothing is recorded. Like voice, a
  shared screen goes through the host's server, which passes it on without
  keeping it.
- **On Linux**, your desktop asks what to share through its screen-sharing
  portal (GNOME, KDE Plasma, Hyprland and sway each have one), and needs
  PipeWire's GStreamer plugin, which the Arch package brings and the .deb
  and .rpm recommend. **The AppImage doesn't share yet**; it watches.
- **A Mac and the phones watch** but don't share, for now.
- If a share can't start or stops by itself, the voice bar says why: the
  room already has three, your computer gave Linger no screen, the picture
  couldn't be read, or the connection to the server dropped.

## Several servers

- **Every server starts folded** when Linger opens (#712), and you open the
  one you want. The first one used to open by itself and push the others out
  of sight. A folded server's header still says what's happening there.
- **Be a different you on each server, for real now** (#719). Settings used
  to change only your first server. Now a **Server** picker at the top of
  Settings says which one you're changing: your name, status and look, your
  password, archive and blocked people, and Hosting, if you host it. Each
  server's **⋯** menu (hold its name on a phone) has **Edit your profile
  here**, and its card in Settings → Servers has **Edit your profile on**
  that server.
- **Less "you're Matt here".** An open server says who you are there only
  when it's news: your status there, or a different name from the one at
  the top of the list.

## Known issues

- **Screen sharing is new.** It was tested on Linux with real screens,
  including a 4K one and windows on other workspaces, on GitHub's Windows
  machines with Windows' own screen capture, and between apps through a real
  server. A PowerPoint shown from a real Windows PC hasn't been tried yet:
  tell us how it goes.
- **The AppImage doesn't share**, a Mac and the phones only watch, and there's
  no sound from the computer.
- **A black slide held on a whole screen** for five seconds gets the "your
  share went black" note too, once a share: Linger doesn't ask whether you
  shared one window or the whole screen.

## Older apps and servers

Nothing stops working with an older app or server (PROTOCOL §9).

- **A 0.5.1 app on a 0.5.2 server** works as before. It doesn't offer Share
  or Watch, and doesn't show who is sharing.
- **A 0.5.2 app on an older server** works too. It doesn't offer Share or
  Watch there: the server doesn't say it passes screens on. Everything else
  in this release, the Server picker, folded servers and the away bank, is in
  the app and works with any server.
- **To watch somebody's screen**, you, they and the server all need 0.5.2.

## For hosts: updating the server

Server image: `ghcr.io/itsmattguenther/linger:0.5.2` (x86-64 only), also
tagged `0.5` and `latest`.

### What changes for you

- **Screen sharing is on.** It needs no new port and no UDP: it travels on
  the server's own address, through Caddy, like the app does.
- **It costs upload.** Each person watching costs about 1.5 to 6 Mbit/s,
  less for a still slide, more for 60 fps. Eight people watching a
  presentation for an hour is about 7 GB out. The server itself barely
  notices: on the smallest DigitalOcean server, eight watchers of a busy
  60 fps screen took about 4% of the CPU.
- **To turn it off**, put `LINGER_SCREENS=off` in `.env` and run
  `docker compose up -d`. Voice carries on; the apps stop offering Share and
  Watch.
- **No database change**, and nothing in `compose.yaml` changes.

### The steps

On the server, in your `linger` folder:

```bash
cd ~/linger
./update.sh
```

(Use `sudo ./update.sh` if you type `sudo` before `docker` commands.) It
downloads 0.5.2 while the server keeps running, stops it for a few seconds to
save the database into `backups/`, then starts everything again and prints the
version before and after. If 0.5.2 doesn't start, it prints the commands that
put 0.5.1 back.

Check it worked, with your own address in place of `linger.example.com`:

```bash
curl -f https://linger.example.com/api/v1/health
```

It prints `{"ok":true,"version":"0.5.2"}`.

**Going back to 0.5.1** is safe: the database is the same, so the commands
`update.sh` prints are all it takes.

## Install or update

Use the in-app update: **Update…** at the foot of the list, or Settings →
Account & App → Updates. Windows users should keep the same installer format
(`.exe` or `.msi`) when upgrading. Linux packages are `.AppImage`, `.deb` and
`.rpm`; on Omarchy or Arch, Linger updates with your system (Update in the
Omarchy menu, or `sudo pacman -Syu`). Windows installers are updater-signed,
not Authenticode-signed; SmartScreen may still warn about an unfamiliar app.

On Android, download `Linger_0.5.2_android-arm64.apk` from this release on
the phone and install it over the old one. You stay signed in. A first install
is step by step in the [0.4.8 notes](0.4.8.md#the-phone-app). On an iPhone,
TestFlight offers 0.5.2 once it's through Apple's processing.

## Building from source

Building the desktop app on Linux now needs GStreamer's headers:
`libgstreamer1.0-dev` and `libgstreamer-plugins-base1.0-dev` on Debian and
Ubuntu (see `docs/development.md`).
